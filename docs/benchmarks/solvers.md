# Same instances, same clock: ALNS, PyVRP, VROOM, jsprit

One thread each, 10 s per Solomon instance and 60 s per 1000-customer instance, seed 1. Every plan is re-checked by this repository's verifier on the instance's own distances; *verified* means feasible and complete. Solomon ranks vehicles first, then distance (best known: SINTEF TOP); Gehring-Homberger (DIMACS rounding) and X (CVRPLIB rounding) rank distance only (best known: CVRPLIB). Produced by `python benchmark/solvers_study.py`.

How to read it. Every solver runs with its default search settings (VROOM at exploration level 5), not a tuned setup. The other solvers minimise cost, so on Solomon they are told to save vehicles by a fixed cost of 10^5 per vehicle; ALNS has a phase of its own for that (guided ejection), which is what the vehicle column shows. Where distance alone ranks (Gehring-Homberger, X), PyVRP is ahead of everything here. Budgets are wall clock on a shared 4-core machine with other jobs running, so a rerun moves individual rows, not the picture; jsprit's time is measured inside the JVM and can overrun its budget on 1000 customers.
