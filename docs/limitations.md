# Limitations and forbidden claims

## Not proven / PARTIAL in v0.1.1

- Improvement of Moscow social taxi KPIs  
- Compatibility with Мосавтосантранс production APIs  
- Optimality of greedy / beam / online insertion  
- True large-scale ALNS, LBBD, or rolling-horizon composition (stubs remain)  
- Stochastic travel times (deterministic buffers only)  
- Fairness as a single score  
- Personal-data certification  
- Vehicle shortage resolution  
- Full passenger/driver mobile apps  

Pooling in v0.1.1 is **classic pickup/dropoff insertion** into a stop sequence, independently feasibility-checked. It is not a proven optimal shareability network.
